Our vision

A world where every organization plans with forecasts it can verify, understand and act on.

Modern forecasting — from classical statistics to time series foundation models — has become remarkably capable. Yet most teams still plan on gut feel or a single brittle number. TrustCast exists to close that gap: we sell a decision, not a technique.

01

Honest uncertainty

Ranges, not just point estimates. You see how confident the forecast is before you commit to it.

02

Decisions, not models

We translate forecasts into the choice in front of you — how much, when, and what if — in plain language.

03

A public track record

Every forecast is scored against what actually happened. Trust is earned in the open, one horizon at a time.

How it works

From your history to a decision brief.

  1. 1

    Share your data

    Send the historical series you plan with, plus the decision you need to make.

  2. 2

    We model and test

  3. 3
