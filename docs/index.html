Time Series Foundation Model Research Center

Which forecasting model should you trust?

TrustCast is an independent research center that tests, adapts and explains time series foundation models — the pretrained AI models that forecast almost any series without training.

See the scorecards Join the founding group

New models arrive every few months. No one independently tells users which one to trust for their data.

Vendors grade their own work

Each model's authors choose the benchmarks they report.

Leaderboards are general

They score public datasets, not the series a central bank, utility or retailer actually forecasts.

Models turn over fast

Practitioners can't keep up with a new release every few months.

Vision

Every organization that forecasts knows which model to trust for its data, and how much to trust each forecast.

Mission

To be the trusted, independent source of evidence on how time series foundation models perform in real domains, and to help organizations turn those forecasts into better decisions.

Neutral by design

Every model runs under the same rules, and results are published whether or not they favor any vendor.

Calibrated uncertainty

Native quantiles, conformal prediction, ensemble intervals and ongoing calibration checks.

Open methods

Evaluation code, curated datasets and methods are public, so anyone can check our work.

Four programs

One shared data and evaluation pipeline, four public outputs.

01 · Flagship

Independent evaluation hub

Tests every major model side by side on domain data. Produces monthly scorecards of accuracy and interval reliability.

02

Domain adaptation research

Fine-tunes open-weight models on curated domain data and measures when it pays off. Produces papers, code and adapted weights.

03

Ensembles & decision science

Combines foundation models with classical baselines and links forecast accuracy to decision quality.

04

Education & access

A hosted sandbox to run every model on your own data, plus workshops, tutorials and a companion textbook.

Roadmap

Form the core group, prove it in one domain, then expand.

  1. Stage 0 · Next 3 months

    Founding

    Form the core group, agree on charter and governance, choose a host or affiliate institution, begin recruiting.

  2. Stage 1 · Year one

    Prove it in one domain

    Build the macro dataset and pipeline, publish the first scorecard, move to monthly updates, then release the first paper, open the sandbox and add a second domain.

  3. Stage 2 · Years two–three

    Expand

    Cover three to five domains, launch fine-tuning and partner benchmarks, and publish regularly.
